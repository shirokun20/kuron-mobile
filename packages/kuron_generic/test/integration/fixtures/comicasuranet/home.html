Asura Scans - The Best Free Manga Website

A Quick History of Asura Scans

Asura Scans started as a small scanlation group, offering quick translations of popular manhwa like "Solo Leveling" and "Nano Machine." Founded in 2011, it has grown to become a well-known name in the scanlation community. By 2022, it became a popular place for action-packed webtoons. It worked under websites like asurascans.com and later asuracomic.net, both of which became very famous.

The platform is run anonymously, most likely by volunteers or hobbyists. The original admin reportedly stepped down in 2023 due to legal issues, which caused concern in the community. Since then, no official team or business has claimed ownership.

What Type of Content Does Asura Scans Offer?

Asura Scans became well-known for its selected collection of Korean manhwa, Chinese manhua, and some Japanese manga. The genres include:

Action / Martial Arts
Fantasy & Isekai
Apocalypse & Regression

Regular updates, clear text formatting, and faster release speed helped keep readers coming back.

This combination made the site popular with binge readers, although legal issues were always a concern.

Is Asura Scans Legal or Piracy?

Asura Scans does not possess licensing rights for the works it translates and distributes. This is called unauthorized copying, or piracy. Hosting or downloading these works can break international copyright laws.

Even though readers often say they use it because there are no official translations, courts usually do not accept this as a valid reason.

Is Asurascans.com down? What happened to Asura Scans?

Early 2025, many Asura Scans fans felt confused when they couldn’t access the site and started asking: Is Asura Scans down?

A Reddit user named RoiHurlemort shared a letter from Asura Scans on the r/manhwa subreddit. The letter said that the eponymously named founder and leader of the site Asura has decided to step down from their position as leader of Asurascans.com. The reason given was “future legal concerns.”

By mid-2025, asurascans.com was confirmed to be down, and asuracomic.net was created to replace it, though with some changes and only partial functionality.

Fans React to Asuracomic.net Changes

A Reddit user, PhilosophyEmpty1010, posted on r/Manhua criticizing the site for adding a 5-second wait between chapters and locking some content behind a paywall. They called this update “the worst” and said it’s wrong for a site without legal rights to try to make money. They also warned that these changes could cause many users to stop using the site.

A Facebook user from Umbra's Hideout warned that it’s time to leave Asura Scans because they introduced a paid subscription for an ad-free experience and early access by 6 hours. They believe this move might cause Webtoon to take stronger actions against Asura Scans. The subscription is sold as a yearly plan with no refunds, which is risky since many manhwas get dropped suddenly. The user also mentioned concerns that Asura Scans could be shut down by Webtoon or other official groups, and subscribers might lose their money.

Additionally, some people on Reddit reported being banned from the Asura Scans Discord server after criticizing the new paid subscription.
